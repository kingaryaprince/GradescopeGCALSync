# Gradescope → Calendar Sync

A Chrome extension that puts your Gradescope due dates on Google Calendar.

It reads Gradescope using the session you are **already signed in to**, so there is no password
to type, store, or leak.

- **No password, ever.** Uses your existing browser session.
- **All your courses**, grouped by term. Pick them from a list.
- **Updates instead of duplicating.** Move a deadline and the event moves with it.
- **Reminders** 24 hours and 1 hour before, configurable.
- **Background sync** every few hours, no tab required.

## Privacy

This extension has no server, no analytics, and no account. Your data goes from Gradescope to
your own Google Calendar and nowhere else.

Read the full [Privacy Policy](privacy.md).
